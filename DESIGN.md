# CarInsurancer Design

## Colors

Use a dark green background (`#111814`), soft beige panels (`#f5eddf`), cream inputs (`#fffcf6`), and green actions (`#245d3c`). Use system sans-serif and rounded controls. Styles live in [app/globals.css](app/globals.css).

## Layout

Use three steps: **Your car → Your coverage → Review selections**. Give each step one primary action. Show a large transparent car image on selection and a compact preview afterward. Stack fields and liability details on narrow screens.

## Quote comparison

Show entered quotes above the form. Keep insurer links separate from quote entry. Preserve quotes when selections change and flag them for recheck. Let users reconfirm coverage without retyping the price or term.
